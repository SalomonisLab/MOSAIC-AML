Objection: "you only ever tested this on BeatAML"

Transferred frozen to an independent cohort

Patient-grouped CV and a sealed hold-out protect against learning a patient; neither protects against learning a cohort. The Cox coefficients, PCA rotation, variable-gene selection and NNLS fusion weights were loaded unchanged and applied to TCGA-LAML — a different platform (Illumina HiSeq RSEM), different institutions, an earlier treatment era. Only the per-gene z-reference was cohort-matched, because RSEM log2 and BeatAML units are not the same scale and transferring without that step measures the platform rather than the patient.

Can it tell one person how long they have?

Group timing works; individual timing does not

Group median survival is accurate to about six weeks. A single patient's is not predictable. Conformal prediction intervals achieve exact coverage, and the honest 80% interval spans 22.6 years — irreducible uncertainty in the underlying data, not a tuning failure. This is why the deployed output leads with restricted mean survival, horizon probabilities and a risk group, and never with a single number of months.

Where should this not be trusted

Stated limits, measured rather than assumed

LimitEvidence
Near-useless without intensive inductionC-index 0.724 in the 284 intensively treated patients, 0.554 in the 71 who were not — where age + ELN is itself below chance (0.481).
Not causal about treatmentBaseline induction type is a covariate, which removes treatment as a confounder. It does not license counterfactuals: 529 of 535 received the same standard induction, and induction intensity partly encodes clinician judgement about fitness.
Treatment gain is externally unvalidatedTCGA-LAML is treatment-homogeneous, so the +0.035 C-index that induction type contributes in BeatAML cannot be checked there at all.
Single-cell survival is a bridge, not a fitThe atlas has vital status for 0 samples. Single-cell patients are reached only through the bulk-equivalent bridge, which is not itself validated against observed survival.
Refuses thin inputBelow 80% gene coverage the risk group starts to flip (percentile drift 0.06 → 0.23 at 75%), so the layer refuses rather than returning a confident number.
Assumes AMLA healthy pooled-CD34 control once scored at the 97th percentile. Bulk input, which has no healthy-vs-diseased gate, now says so explicitly on the report.
