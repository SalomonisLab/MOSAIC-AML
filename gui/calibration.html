Objection: "your thresholds were tuned to flatter the model"

Re-deriving the call thresholds fixed the over-calling

With the old Youden thresholds (grey, dotted), call accuracy collapsed to ~27–37% in the 0.7–0.9 band — the model over-called, and mid-to-high-probability calls were mostly wrong. Re-deriving each mutation's threshold to maximise F1 (gold) lifts that band to ~69–91% and overall held-out call accuracy from 0.73 → 0.86.
Objection: "a reported probability is not a probability"

What the model reports versus what is true

The red line is the confidence the model reports (its raw probability). The gold line is the actual chance the mutation is present. The shaded gap is the over-confidence: a call reported at 0.85 is truly present only ~26% of the time. Calibration replaces the red claim with the gold reality — that gold value is the "chance ✓" the board now shows. The bigger the gap, the more calibration changes.
