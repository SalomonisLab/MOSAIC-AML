What this does not establish

Limitations, stated plainly

  • Ex vivo is not clinical benefit. BeatAML measured viability of mononuclear cells in culture. Pharmacokinetics, the marrow microenvironment, combination therapy and toxicity are not represented. Nothing here is validated against patient outcomes.
  • The state-resolved layer has no ground truth. No assay measured per-cell-state response, so Model B is checked against a pre-registered biological expectation and internal consistency, not accuracy.
  • "Blast compartment" is a compartment, not a malignant-cell call. No per-cell somatic genotype is used.
  • Combinations are coverage hypotheses. BeatAML2 contains single-agent measurements only; no synergy is estimated, and single-agent scores are never added.
  • 47 of the 165 screened inhibitors are excluded for too few specimens, no dynamic range, degenerate response tails or an acquisition-wave shift. Three more (Cytarabine, Nutlin 3a, GDC-0941) are wave-unstable and are reported only with that caveat attached.
  • Single-cell input is an assay transfer. The model is fitted on bulk BeatAML and applied to single-cell bulk-equivalents through cohort-matched references; that transfer is not itself validated against measured ex-vivo response in single-cell-profiled patients.
