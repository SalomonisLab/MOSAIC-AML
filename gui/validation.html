Which model for which data

Two models × four cohorts

Match the model to the assay. The bulk RNA-only model (BeatAML-trained) is strong on bulk (AUROC 0.83–0.86) but collapses on single-cell (0.70); the multimodal model is single-cell-native (0.88) and cannot run on bulk cohorts (no single-cell modalities). They are complementary, not competing.

Objection: "the multimodal gain is just RNA re-imputed"

Most of the gain is independent of RNA-imputation

~69% of the bulk→multimodal AUROC gain needs no imputed modalities. Dropping the four RNA-imputed blocks (ADT, Lipid, Metabolite, GRN) keeps most of the advantage — so it is real single-cell information, not circular re-use of RNA.

Objection: "is the weak-mutation signal real?"

Every mutation clears chance

All 24 mutations' 95% CIs sit above 0.5 and every one beats a 1,000-shuffle permutation null (p ≤ 0.003) — including the low-precision ones (KRAS, RUNX1, IDH2). Weak F1 reflects rarity, not absence of signal.

Objection: "your threshold was picked on the same data"

Honest operating point (nested CV)

Choosing the threshold on held-out folds removes the optimism: mean F1 0.44 → 0.34, sensitivity 0.51 → 0.45, specificity 0.96 → 0.94. The nested numbers are what we report.
PDF
Objection: "are the misses real failures?"

Misses are largely subclonal

Sensitivity rises monotonically with variant allele frequency — 0.29 (subclonal <0.10) → 0.69 (clonal ≥0.40). Misses concentrate at low VAF: an expression-detection limit, not random error.
